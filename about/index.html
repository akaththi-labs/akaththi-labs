About Akaththi Labs

Akaththi Labs is the automation and technology unit of Akaththi Farms Private Limited, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Three units

Akaththi Farms
Grows and sells mushrooms and microgreens in Chennai.
Akaththi Labs
Builds and operates the technology: POD, Kaani Intelligence and managed farms.
Akaththi Research
Does the science: cultivation, strains and new cooling methods. Hands proven results to Labs.

How we work

  • Prove it on our own farm before we offer it.
  • Measure before we claim.
  • Standard procedures, measured waste, steady improvement.
