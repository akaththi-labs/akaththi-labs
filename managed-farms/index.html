The problem

  • Most new farms fail on know-how, not on ambition: hygiene, spawn and seed handling, timing.
  • Good inputs are hard to source on time and at steady quality.
  • Building a controlled farm costs a lot before the first sale.
  • Hiring and training skilled operators is slow, and they are hard to keep.

Who does what

  1. Our procedures. The standard operating procedures we run our own farm by, and the advanced farming technology behind them.
  2. Our supplies. We supply the raw materials and keep the inventory stocked.
  3. Our operators. We operate the farm day to day.
  4. Your market. You own the harvest and sell it, in your space or your channels.

Before a pilot

Food-safety licensing and who is responsible for what are agreed in writing before any pilot starts.
