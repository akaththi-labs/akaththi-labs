Three ways to take part

  1. Technology lease. A POD, Kaani and our growing procedures for a monthly fee. Your team grows.
  2. Managed farm. The same, and our operators run the farm to our procedures. You pay an operations fee.
  3. Managed, with offtake. The same, and Akaththi Farms can buy or help sell your harvest, under a separate agreement.

Who keeps what

The harvest is yours. You decide whether to use it, sell it or sell it to Akaththi Farms. That choice never sits inside the technology contract.

Food-safety licensing and who is responsible for what are agreed in writing before any pilot starts.

We take on a small number of pilots we can support properly.
