Built on a working farm

Akaththi Farms grows and sells exotic mushrooms in Chennai. Every Labs product runs there before it goes anywhere else. If it does not hold up on our farm, we do not offer it.

Three units share the work, in this order:

  1. Akaththi Research proves an idea works, with measurements.
  2. Akaththi Labs turns it into something others can build, buy and run.
  3. Akaththi Farms runs it every day and tells us what breaks.
