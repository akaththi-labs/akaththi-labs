What breaks on farms today

Mushrooms we grow
Climate drifts, so batches abort or get contaminated. In hot places, growing all year needs costly cooling. Harvest and packing stay hand work.
Microgreens we grow
Mould follows excess moisture and poor airflow. Shelf life is short, so supply has to match demand closely. Picking and packing is the biggest variable labour cost.
Saffron under study
Every flower is picked by hand in a short autumn bloom. Cool, steady conditions are hard to hold outside Kashmir. Adulteration erodes trust in the product.
Vertical farming
Energy and labour dominate operating cost. Several well-funded farms built at scale before their unit economics worked, and failed.
Farm software
Every device and tool keeps its own data. Growers still piece the farm together from clipboards, chat messages and spreadsheets.

How we avoid those mistakes

One farm first

We prove the economics on our own farm before we build more. Scale comes after proof, not before.

Energy by design

Energy is a design limit from day one, not a bill we look at later.

Machines help people

Automation removes guesswork from the operator's day. It does not pretend people are not needed.
